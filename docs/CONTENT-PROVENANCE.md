# Content provenance and review status

The first-release profile descriptions, lesson prompts and learning guide prose were authored for LangSpot. They are not copied textbook chapters. Dataset records include a provenance/review label. Initial draft does not mean specialist-verified.

The vocabulary entries are a small starter set intended to demonstrate the data model. Some transliteration conventions are learner-oriented and may vary by dialect, scholarly tradition or romanization standard. Pāli and Sanskrit examples are illustrative and should be checked against reliable grammars and editions before citation in scholarly work.

External references are registered as links only. A public URL is not permission to redistribute the page's contents. For new external records, document the author/creator, exact source, license, attribution, modifications and date accessed when relevant.
